# Ghostfolio — Demo Video Script (< 3 minutes)

Target length: **~2:40**. Record in English (narration lines below). Record on the
live site: https://ketong112.github.io/ghostfolio/
Use a screen recorder (Chromebook: Ctrl+Shift+Overview, or the Screen Capture tool).
Speak clearly, one sentence per shot. No music (copyright). No facecam needed.

---

## 0:00–0:15 — The problem (show: live site, empty portfolio)
**On screen:** Ghostfolio homepage, empty state.
**Say:** "Memecoin traders hold dozens of tokens — and most of them die. Ghostfolio
is a haunted portfolio tracker that shows you, in seconds, what is alive, what is
dying, and what is already a ghost."

## 0:15–0:50 — Add a live holding (show: AddHolding form)
**On screen:** Select Solana, paste a live memecoin contract address, enter an
amount, click "Summon holding".
**Say:** "Paste a contract address — Solana, BNB Chain, or Ethereum — and an amount.
Ghostfolio prices it live from DexScreener. No wallet, no sign-up, nothing leaves
your browser."

## 0:50–1:20 — The living portfolio (show: portfolio table + summary)
**On screen:** The holding in the table with price, value, 24h change, "Alive" status.
Point at the summary (total value, alive/dying/ghost counts).
**Say:** "Living holdings are friendly spirits. The summary shows total value and
24h change — and the fog thickens as your portfolio gets more volatile."

## 1:20–1:55 — The graveyard (show: a dead token as a ghost)
**On screen:** Scroll to the graveyard. A dead token's ghost card with RIP date.
**Say:** "When a token's liquidity dries up, its volume disappears, or its price
collapses 95 percent from its peak — it dies, and joins the graveyard as a ghost,
with the date it died. This is the whole point: how much of your money is in dead
coins, at a glance."

## 1:55–2:20 — A wail (show: Recent wails list)
**On screen:** The "Recent wails" list (and, if one fires live, the banner).
**Say:** "When a holding drops sharply or loses liquidity fast, its ghost wails —
a banner, an animation, and an entry here with a timestamp. The wail IS the alert."

## 2:20–2:40 — Close (show: footer + repo link)
**On screen:** Footer ("Prices by DexScreener"), then open the GitHub repo briefly.
**Say:** "Ghostfolio was built spec-first with the Kiro CLI — specs, steering docs,
and hooks, all in the repo. Open source, MIT licensed. Happy haunting."

---

## Before recording — checklist
- [ ] Open the live site in a clean browser window (close extra tabs).
- [ ] Have 2 contract addresses ready: one LIVE memecoin (Solana) and one DEAD/rugged
      token (so the graveyard is not empty). Add both BEFORE recording, so the video
      shows a full portfolio + graveyard.
- [ ] Turn sound OFF in the app (the wail sound is optional; keep the video quiet).
- [ ] Record at 1080p if possible. Keep the mouse still when not clicking.
- [ ] After recording, trim the start/end. Check the final file is UNDER 3:00.
- [ ] Upload to YouTube as Public, title: "Ghostfolio — Haunted Crypto Portfolio Tracker (Kiroween 2026)".
